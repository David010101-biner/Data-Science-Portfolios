## 📂 Featured Projects

### 1. Customer Personality Analysis & Predictive Marketing
* **Industry Domain:** E-Commerce & Retail Marketing
* **Objective:** Maximize marketing ROI by segmenting customers based on purchasing behavior and predicting their response to future promotional campaigns.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python`, `Pandas`, `Scikit-Learn` (K-Means Clustering, Random Forest Classifier), `Seaborn`
* **Key Achievements:** 
  * Developed a segmentation model to categorize customer profiles.
  * Built a classification system to accurately predict campaign acceptance rates based on historical spending data.

### 2. Credit Risk Prediction & Default Scoring
* **Industry Domain:** Financial Technology (Fintech) & Risk Mitigation
* **Objective:** Build an intelligent scoring system to evaluate financial profiles and predict the probability of loan defaults, aiming to reduce Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratios.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python`, `Scikit-Learn` (Balanced Random Forest), Data Imputation, Outlier Handling
* **Key Achievements:** 
  * Handled highly imbalanced financial datasets using class weighting techniques.
  * Extracted actionable insights (Feature Importance) proving that the Debt-to-Income ratio is the most critical factor in credit default risks.

### 3. HR & People Analytics: Employee Turnover Prediction
* **Industry Domain:** Human Resource Management & People Analytics
* **Objective:** Identify the structural root causes of employee attrition (turnover) and build a predictive model to retain top talent.
* **Tech Stack:** `Python`, `Pandas`, `Matplotlib`, `Machine Learning Classification`
* **Key Achievements:** 
  * Cleaned and engineered HR master data (One-Hot Encoding for categorical HR metrics).
  * Provided a data-driven retention strategy highlighting compensation structure and excessive overtime as the primary drivers of burnout and resignation.
